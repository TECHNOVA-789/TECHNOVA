The lost and found portal is a web based platform designed to help people and to find their lost belongings.

#main features

*Report lost items, search for missing belongings,track the status of reported items

#Benefits

*Save time and efforts,reduce the chances of losing valuable belonging permanently, creates a helpful and secure community

#conclusion

*The lost found portal is a smart and helpful platform that makes it easier to find missing items and return them in their owner,it saves time, reduce stress and uses technology to connect people
